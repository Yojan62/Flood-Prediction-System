The Critical Issue: Peak Flood Failure

Despite its high overall score, the model **fails** at its most important job: predicting extreme flood peaks. The R2 score for the top 10% of flood events is negative, meaning the model is consistently and dangerously **under-predicting** the severity of major floods.

Conclusion & Future Work

The model is a powerful tool for predicting general flow but is **not suitable for real-world flood alerts** due to its failure at predicting peaks. This critical issue is caused by **missing upstream data**: the model can't see a "wave" of water coming from a dam or snowmelt until it's too late. Future work must focus on integrating new data sources, such as upstream river gauges, to give the model the "eyes" it needs to see large-scale flood events coming.
